## Zeta 0.6.5

- **Library recovery.** Zeta keeps up to three automatic library snapshots. Preview one before restoring it, undo your last Zeta import, or send a backup to another device through Wayship.
- **Radio.** Build a station from two or three songs. Recommendations stay tied to the original seeds as the station refills. You can see why a song was picked, save the station, or start an offline mix from downloaded tracks.
- **Playback.** Weak connection mode temporarily tries smaller streams after repeated slow loads. Listening anniversaries show what you played on this day last year.
- **Desktop.** The window buttons and rounded corners work on KDE. With Minimize to tray enabled, closing the window keeps playback running and the tray icon reopens Zeta.
- **Sharing and imports.** Copy Zeta track links from the track menu. YouTube playlist import can recover tracks from another source when its first lookup fails.
- **Scrobbling.** Enter your own Last.fm API credentials in Settings. ListenBrainz continues to use your personal user token.
- **Discord.** `/defaultdevice` saves your preferred player, and `/nowplaying` shares artwork and playback progress.

The Android APK uses the fork's signing key. Back up your library before replacing an upstream installation signed with another key.
