## Zeta 0.6.3

- Wayship keeps the saved account through authorization failures and retries the connection. Settings can reconnect an account whose token has been revoked.
- Desktop Zeta installs and updates a verified copy of yt-dlp automatically.
- Zeta Discord keeps the playback, voice sharing, lyrics, and listening commands. The bot keeps its Discord connection ready to acknowledge commands promptly.

The fork's Android APK uses a separate signing key. Back up your library before replacing an upstream Android installation.
